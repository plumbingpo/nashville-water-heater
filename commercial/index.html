Kitchen Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and a high-temperature dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Nashville Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we schedule visits around your operating hours.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize at peak service. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, keeping downtime short, and keeping part of the system online whenever the layout allows.

Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

After-Hours Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Nashville Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Nashville and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, get hot water flowing again, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

A maintenance program from Nashville Water Heater gets built around your equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. Visits include tank flushing and anode checks, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the tap. Mineral-heavy water cuts the life of commercial equipment fast, so busy properties often benefit from more frequent visits.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
